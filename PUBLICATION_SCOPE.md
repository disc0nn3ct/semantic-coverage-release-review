# Public artifact scope

This is a code/data release of the replacement controlled study. It is not a
claim of acceptance or publication of the accompanying journal manuscript.

The local source archives are preserved. This public derivative omits only:

1. the frozen earlier manuscript SOURCE archive;
2. the journal template ZIP and its extracted demonstration assets;
3. administrative document-generation, editorial-response and annotation-packet
   copies that are not inputs to the reported computations.

Publication manifests list every omitted archive member and original member hash.
Retained source patches, corpus/split records, model outputs, scores, vectors,
statistical results and original scientific protocols retain their source bytes.
The historical synthetic-study audit is clearly separate from the replacement
study. It is retained for transparency and contributes no replacement observations.

Original scientific metadata may contain research-host Windows paths and version
strings. These records are preserved to retain the meaning of frozen hashes;
portable replay commands pass explicit local paths. No actual authentication
secrets were identified by the publication scan. The package includes public
upstream contributor metadata and source-license notices.

Archived internal manifests describe the original sealed source snapshots. They
are not silently rewritten to imply that omitted publication-scope files were
never present. Use PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.json and ARCHIVES_SHA256SUMS.txt for the
public archive identity and retain the original source identities for provenance.

No model weights, private journal messages, completed human annotation records,
or personal working-directory dumps are included. There are no completed
independent human judgments: model responses and blank packets do not supply them.
